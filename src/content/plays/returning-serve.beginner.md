A serve that's coming at you quickly doesn't need a big swing. Its pace is already there. Get your racket back early, then a short, firm swing is enough to send it back.

Aim cross-court, low over the net, at the server. After serving, they run in to join their partner at the net, so a low ball arrives around their feet while they're still moving. From there they can only lift it back up, and a ball that comes back up to you is an easy one to play.

### Watch out for

- **Hitting it at the net player.** Their partner is already at the net, straight in front of you. A return down the line is a gift to them.
- **Hitting it high and flat.** A return that's neither low nor a proper lob sits up at the server's shoulder, and they'll volley it away.
