---
play: Lob deep, then go up together
shots: [Lob, Volley]
---

The lob is the easiest way to the net. It stays in the air longer than any other shot, so it gives both of you time to move. Send it high and cross-court, over the player on the diagonal, to land beyond their service line and before their back glass. Cross-court is the longest line on the court, so it leaves you the most room for error.

Then watch it. Once it's over them and they've turned to chase it, walk up together, side by side. Stop as they're about to hit, level with the second post if you've got that far, and send their reply back deep. You've got the net now, and keeping it is the job.

### When not to

If the lob drops short, around their service line, they'll take it in the air. Stay back. If you go up anyway, you're caught halfway with the ball at your feet.

### Watch out for

- **Rushing it.** Be patient at the back. Keep defending until you get the lob that works, rather than jumping from defending straight to going for winners.
- **Going up alone.** If only one of you moves, the middle is open. Go together or not at all.
