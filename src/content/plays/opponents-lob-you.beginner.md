---
play: Let it bounce, then lob it back
shots: [Lob]
---

Taking a lob in the air is hard to do well, and a missed overhead hands them the point. If you let it bounce, it comes off the back glass slower and lower, and you can hit it at a comfortable height.

The lob you send back matters more than it looks. Make it high and deep, so they have to turn and chase it. Their chase is the time you need to walk back up to the net together.

### Watch out for

- **Standing too close to the glass.** Leave a big step between you and the back wall, so the ball has room to come out to you.
- **Lobbing short.** A lob that lands near their service line is an easy smash. If in doubt, go higher and deeper.
