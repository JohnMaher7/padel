The middle is the biggest target on the court. It's 5 m from both side walls, so it gives you the most room for error, and it asks nothing clever of you. The rest comes free: they have to work out whose ball it is, and because it's in the middle, their reply has little angle and comes back to where you're waiting.

Depth matters as much as direction. Follow the centre line and aim to land the ball just past their service line, where the two lines meet. Hit it unhurried, so it's dying by the time it reaches their back glass and gives them nothing to attack.

### Watch out for

- **Hitting it hard or high.** A ball that comes quickly off their back glass, or sits up off it, hands them an easy one. Deep and slow beats hard.
- **Leaving a gap between you.** Their reply comes back through the middle, so it belongs to whoever has the forehand there. You both move a step in while it's in the air, so nothing gets through between you.
