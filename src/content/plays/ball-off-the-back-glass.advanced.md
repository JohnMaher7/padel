Make the same read and give it the same room. But when the ball comes out between knee and waist height, you don't have to lob. Play it soft and low, cross-court, so it dips at the feet of the player at the net diagonally across. It's about where it lands, not how hard it goes.

From down at their feet, they can only lift their volley. That's your cue: you and your partner move in together while their ball floats back, and the point has turned round.

### When not to

If the ball comes out low and fast and you're cramped, there's no time to place a soft shot. Play the Beginner lob instead.

### Watch out for

- **Hitting it too hard.** It's a placement shot. Soft and low is the whole point.
- **Moving in alone.** If only one of you goes forward, their volley goes through the gap. Go together, or stay back together.
