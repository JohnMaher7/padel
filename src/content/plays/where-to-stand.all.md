---
play: Know your spot before the serve
shots: [Volley]
---

Who's serving decides where everyone starts. The serving team starts with one player at the net and one at the back, and the server joins their partner at the net after a good serve. The receiving team starts with both players back, where there's time to see the serve and return it.

The landmarks are there so you don't have to guess. Glance at the side of the court, find the post or the glass join, and stand level with it. It's the same spot every point, whatever your level.

### Watch out for

- **Receiving from inside the service line.** It's allowed, but from there a good serve is very hard to handle.
- **Hugging the net when your partner serves.** In front of the second post, a lob gets over you easily.
- **One up and one back when you receive.** Start level with your partner, then move up or back together.
