---
play: Middle first, then angle it into the gap
shots: [Volley]
---

This takes the initiative without risking the first ball. The first volley goes deep down the middle, which is the safe ball. To cover it, both of them move in towards the middle, and that leaves both sides of their court empty. The second volley goes into the space they left: angled short and wide, so it runs on into their side mesh.

The first ball doesn't win the point; it sets it up. The second one, into the gap, is the one that wins it.

### When not to

If the first ball is high and easy, don't bother with the middle: angle it away straight off. If their reply comes back low, at your feet, go down the middle again rather than forcing the angle. Save the angle for a ball you can attack.

### Watch out for

- **A left-hander on their right.** With a right-hander on the left, both their forehands meet in the middle, so it's their strongest spot. Play wide against them instead.
- **Going there every time.** If every ball goes down the middle, they'll wait for it. Mix it with cross-court and straight, so they can't read you.
