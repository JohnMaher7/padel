---
play: Lob it over the server, and take the net
shots: [Lob]
---

The serving team wants to be at the net, together. After serving, the server runs in to get there. A high, deep lob cross-court over their head sends them straight back to where they started, and it gives you the time to run in and take the net instead. One shot swaps the teams round.

The lob has to be deep: aim for it to land between their service line and the back glass. A short lob is an easy smash. Cross-court gives you the most length, and so the most room for error.

### When not to

If the serve is tight into the glass and you're struggling just to reach it, don't try anything clever. Block it back to the server and live to play another shot.

### Watch out for

- **Lobbing and staying back.** The lob only works if you both move in behind it. Watching it from the back gives the net back to them.
- **Always doing the same thing.** If you lob every return, the server stops running in. Mix in the low return to their feet to keep them guessing.
