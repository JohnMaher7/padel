---
play: Take it early with a bandeja, and keep the net
shots: [Bandeja]
---

Giving up the net is exactly what the lob wants. If the ball is going to drop around your service line, you can take it in the air instead, with a bandeja: a short, sliced overhead that sends the ball deep rather than hard.

A bandeja isn't meant to win the point. It keeps you at the net. Aim it deep, towards the middle or their backhand corner, so it stays low off the glass and they have to defend again.

### When to let it go

If the lob is deep enough to push you behind the service line, don't hit an overhead while you back-pedal. You won't have the balance to get back in. Let it bounce and play the beginner answer instead.

### Watch out for

- **Hitting it hard.** A fast bandeja comes off their back glass fast, and hands them an easy ball. Control and depth beat pace.
- **Staying where you hit it.** Step straight back in after contact, or your partner is left at the net alone.
