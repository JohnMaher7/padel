## When to use it

The lob is the main way to get from the back of the court to the net. A high, deep ball over the net players makes them turn and chase it, and while they do, you and your partner move up and take the net. When you're under pressure, it also buys you time.

Play it on a ball you're comfortable with: one that's dropping after the bounce, or coming off the back glass. Stretched in a corner or off balance, a lob tends to land short, so keep the ball in play another way. And if they're already set to smash, or expecting the lob, save it for later.

## Step by step

1. **Get ready early.** Continental grip, racket back and turned side-on, just as for any other shot from the back.
2. **Get low.** Set your feet wide and bend your knees, so you're underneath the ball.
3. **Open the racket face.** Tilt it to face the sky, and drop the racket below the ball.
4. **Meet it in front, underneath.** Hit the bottom of the ball in front of you, around waist height or lower. Keep your grip firm all the way through: a loose racket gives a short lob.
5. **Lift with your legs.** Swing smoothly from low to high, and let your legs and body do the lifting, not your wrist.
6. **Finish high.** Let the racket carry on up towards your target. Don't cut the swing short.
7. **Aim deep, and cross-court.** Land it between their service line and the back glass, the deeper the better. A high lob stays closer to the glass after it bounces, which makes it harder to play. Cross-court is the longest line on the court, so it gives you the most room.

## Watch out for

- **Lobbing short.** A lob that lands around their service line is an easy smash. If in doubt, go higher and deeper.
- **Standing up to get height.** The height comes from bending your knees and rising through them, not from straightening up or leaning back.
- **Brushing it with topspin.** A tennis-style topspin lob kicks off the back glass, and a ball that kicks off the glass is easy to attack. Hit flat, under the ball.
- **Lobbing and staying back.** When a lob gets over them, move in together, or the chance to take the net is gone. After a short one, stay back and defend.
