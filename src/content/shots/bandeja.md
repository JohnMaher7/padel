## When to use it

When they lob you at the net, the bandeja lets you take the ball in the air and stay there. It isn't meant to win the point. It's slower and more controlled than a smash, and it goes deep, so they have to defend again. Think of it as a high forehand volley.

Play it on a lob that's too deep to smash away but that you can still take around your service line. A short lob is one to smash. If the lob is going to push you well behind the service line, let it bounce and play it off the back glass instead: every step back makes the way back to the net longer.

## Step by step

1. **Turn side-on straight away.** The moment you read the lob, turn so your other shoulder points at the net.
2. **Get the racket up early.** Continental grip, elbow up, racket above your head with the face open, like carrying a tray.
3. **Move back side-on.** Quick steps back, sideways, not running backwards facing the net. Point your free arm up at the ball: it helps you judge the distance and keeps you balanced.
4. **Meet it in front, at forehead height.** Contact in front of you and a little to your hitting side. Don't let it drop below your head, and don't reach up to full stretch.
5. **Swing high to low.** From a short take-back, with the face slightly open. That puts some slice on the ball and keeps its bounce low, but don't chop under it: it still has to carry deep.
6. **Aim deep, at medium pace.** Cross-court into the corner first, down the middle second. It should land past their service line and reach the glass low, and the slower ball gives you time to get back in.
7. **Finish short, and go back in.** Let the racket come across your body, not down to your knees as in a tennis smash. Then go straight back to the net, level with your partner.

## Watch out for

- **Hitting it hard.** A fast, flat bandeja comes back off their back glass as an easy ball.
- **Letting it get behind you.** Contact on top of you or behind you sends the ball up and tips you backwards, away from the net.
- **Dropping the elbow.** If the elbow or racket drops before you swing, the racket comes up through the ball, and it bounces high.
- **Staying to watch it.** The bandeja only keeps you at the net if you get back in straight away.
