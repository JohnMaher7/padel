## When to use it

The chiquita is the other way to take the net from the back. Instead of going over the net players, it drops at their feet, so they have to volley the ball up. It rarely wins the point; it sets up the next shot.

It needs space in front of them. Play it when they're level with the second post along the side (the net post counts as the first) or further back: about 2 m or more off the net. Any closer and they'll meet it high and attack it, so lob instead. It's easiest on a ball that's come off the back glass, because it's slower, already coming your way, and gives you time to see where they are. Not from right up against the back wall, though: from there it's too far to follow in.

The chiquita and the lob work as a pair. One brings them in, the other goes over them. Mix them, so they can't read you.

## Step by step

1. **Set up as for a lob.** Continental grip, side-on, racket below the ball with the face slightly open. Until the last moment, it should look like a lob.
2. **Get low.** Bend your knees, and rise through your legs as you hit.
3. **Keep the take-back tiny.** Almost none at all.
4. **Let it drop, in front of you.** Wait for the ball to fall to between knee and waist height, and hit it in front of your hip. If it gets behind you, you end up scooping it with your wrist.
5. **Push it gently, flat.** A slow push forward and a little up, like a small lob, not a scoop. Flat is the one to learn first.
6. **Clear the net by about 30 cm, and let it dip.** It should start falling as it reaches the net, so they have to play it below net height. Aim at their feet, down the middle by default.
7. **Follow a good one in.** If it's low, move up to the net together. If it floats up, stay back and defend.

## Watch out for

- **Hitting it too hard.** It's often a tennis habit. A fast chiquita rises, they volley it down, and you can't follow it in.
- **Playing it when they're close to the net.** They'll take it high and attack.
- **Heavy topspin.** A padel racket has no strings to grip the ball, so a tennis-style dipping shot is hard to hit both fast and accurately. Keep it flat and slow.
- **Playing it every time.** They'll start stepping in to cut it off. Mix it with the lob.
