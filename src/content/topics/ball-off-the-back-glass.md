---
title: Ball off the back glass
kind: situation
summary: The ball has got past you and is heading for the back glass. Let it come off, then play it.
draft: true
spot: [2.6, 19.6]
---

- **Read it early.** A fast, flat ball comes a long way out of the glass; a slow or looping one drops close to it. Straight in comes straight out.
- **Turn side-on and give it room.** Shoulder to the side glass, and back off out of the ball's line early, in small steps. For a fast ball, be about level with the join in the side glass, 2 m from the back wall. Closer if it's slow.
- **Let it pass, then step into it.** Hit it in front of you at about waist height, before it bounces again. Stepping forward into a ball is much easier than backing away from one.
- **Guide it, don't hit it.** The glass has already turned the ball round towards the net. Control wins here, not power.
- **Their ball that hits your glass before it bounces is out.** Let it go.
