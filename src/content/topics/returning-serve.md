---
title: Returning serve
kind: situation
summary: They're serving to you. Your return decides which team gets to the net first.
draft: false
spot: [7.1, 18.3]
---

- **Stand where you can move both ways.** About a metre behind your service line, level with the join in the side glass. Too close to the glass and you're cramped; too deep and you're always late.
- **Racket back before it bounces.** A serve arrives quickly. If your racket is already back when the ball lands, you only have to adjust your feet, not rush your swing.
- **Give it room.** If the serve is heading into the side glass, step away and let it come off towards you, rather than squeezing a shot in between the ball and the wall.
- **The return starts the point; it doesn't win it.** Nobody expects a winner. Get it back in, low or high, and cross-court when in doubt: it's the longest line on the court, so it gives you the most room for error.
- **You can't volley a serve.** It has to bounce first. After that, anything goes, including letting it come off the glass.
