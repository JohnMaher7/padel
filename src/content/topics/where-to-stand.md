---
title: Where to stand
kind: situation
summary: At the start of every point, your spot depends on who's serving.
draft: true
spot: [3.2, 15.6]
---

- **Serving: just behind the service line.** Stand in the middle of your half, with a foot behind the line and not on it. After a good serve, follow it in to the net.
- **Your partner serving: at the net, level with the second post.** Count the net post as the first: the second is about 2 m back from the net. Stand a little in from the middle of your half, because the middle is yours to cover.
- **Receiving: level with the join in the side glass.** The join is 2 m from the back wall, about a metre behind your service line. Stand about 2 m in from the side glass, so you can move either way.
- **Your partner receiving: back, level with them.** Not at the net. Start level with each other, then move up or back together.
- **Go by the distances if your court differs.** Court makers space their posts differently, and some courts have no join in the side glass. About 2 m from the net at the net, and about 2 m from the back wall at the back.
