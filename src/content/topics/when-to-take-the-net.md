---
title: When to take the net
kind: situation
summary: You're both back and they hold the net. Your shot decides whether you can swap places.
draft: true
spot: [7.0, 14.2]
---

- **Go up only once your shot has worked.** Your lob is over their heads and they've turned to chase it, or your low ball has dropped at their feet and they have to hit up. If it hasn't, stay back and keep defending. A ball from them that sits up for you is a chance too: take it early and move in behind it.
- **Go together, and stay level.** Move up side by side. One of you up and one back leaves a hole down the middle.
- **Stop as they hit, and split step.** Your shot buys you time to move, and when they're about to hit, that time is up, wherever you are. If you can, get level with the second post, about 2 m from the net. Any closer and a lob gets over you.
- **Don't wait between the service line and the second post.** You may have to stop there for a moment as they hit, but go on through with the next ball, forward or back. Wait in that gap and you're caught halfway, with the ball at your feet.
- **Getting to the net isn't winning the point.** Once you're there, finish only an easy ball. Otherwise volley it deep and keep your place, and if they lob you well, go back together and start again.
