---
title: Opponents lob you
kind: situation
summary: You're both at the net, and they send the ball high over your heads.
draft: false
spot: [3, 12.8]
---

- **Read it before it's hit.** When an opponent drops their racket low and gets under the ball, a lob is coming. Start moving as it leaves their racket, not when it's already over you.
- **Turn side-on, then move.** Running backwards while you face the net is slow, and it's how people fall. Turn your shoulder to the net and shuffle back, keeping your eyes on the ball.
- **Call it.** Say "mine" or "yours" early. Whoever isn't playing it moves too, so the two of you never leave a gap.
- **Get back to the net.** The lob is their way of pushing you off it. Whatever you play back, the aim is for both of you to end up there again.
